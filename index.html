How this works
The app uses the stable ts-fsrs 5.4.2 implementation loaded from jsDelivr when you study. Your cards and review state stay in this browser via localStorage; no account is required. Export/import of progress is available below. The FSRS engine is separate from AlgoApp, so its exact scheduling will not necessarily match AlgoApp's internal settings.
Reset deletes this app's saved scheduling state from this browser only.
